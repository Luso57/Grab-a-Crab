TREASURE CHEST DECORATION
1467 triangles; 39 editable meshes.
Solid-color materials; flat shading; no image textures.

Chest_Lid is one separate mesh including its planks, arched bands, trim, and rivets. Its origin is the rear hinge. Blender local X rotation: 0 degrees = closed; -110 degrees = open. Saved and exported OPEN. Rotation intentionally remains unapplied on this mesh to preserve the open/closed relationship.

Lid/base clearance checked every 5 degrees through the range.
FBX coordinates use the exporter axis conversion; set the imported lid pivot / joint at the corresponding rear hinge when animating in Roblox. Importer pivot behavior and material handling must be checked in Studio. Material colors are also listed in the JSON file.
